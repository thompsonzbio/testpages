Responding to Emerging Pathogen Threats

The Pathogen Genomics Centers of Excellence (PGCoE) Network leads efforts to create the technical and social unification necessary to enable nimble, coordinated work between partners when generating and using sequence data as during an infectious disease event. This work ensures we have consistent ways of generating and capturing genomic and metadata, methods for sharing and/or collating across states, jurisdictions, and institutions, and protocols for how we communicate and work together during an infectious disease response.

Roadmap for Building Genomic Epidemiology Capacity

Looking to build genomics-informed response capacity?

We've developed a roadmap of what expertise, capabilities, and infrastructure is necessary to support sustained, routine application of pathogen genomics to public health questions. Data Sharing Resources

The PGCoE Network is tackling big challenges in data sharing in several ways, including the development of data sharing templates and metadata specifications for genomic epidemiology endpoints. Available documents and templates are listed below.

NCBI Upload Companion: The PGCoE Network developed this resource to assist public health scientists submitting data to National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) databases. Acting as a companion to official NCBI documentation, the guide provides an overview of NCBI's databases and specific recommendations for common issues encountered by public health agencies when uploading genomic data.

Sample Size Calculations

The NE PGCoE developed a work instruction covering the uses of the phylosamp R package for the purpose of sample sizing for viral variant surveillance. Example code, outputs, and detailed instructions are provided to perform power analyses and sample sizing for surveilling viral variants.
